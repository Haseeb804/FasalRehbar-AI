About FasalRehbar AI

Our Mission

To empower Pakistani farmers with advanced technology for crop disease detection and protection.

We believe that every farmer deserves access to modern agricultural technology. FasalRehbar AI combines artificial intelligence with agricultural expertise to provide instant disease detection and expert recommendations.

Our Vision

A future where every crop is protected and every farmer has access to cutting-edge disease detection.

By 2030, we aim to be the leading agricultural disease detection platform in South Asia, helping millions of farmers protect their crops and increase yields.
